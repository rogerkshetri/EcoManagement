EcoManagement

EcoManagement is a website developed by Group OL10 for EcoManagement, a company that provides household energy and water monitoring solutions. Users upload their utility bills to access and manage their consumption information, while EcoManagement sensors collect detailed usage data from the household.

The platform combines these data sources to generate visual bill translations, consumption insights and personalised saving recommendations through a simple and user-friendly interface.

The goal of the platform is to help households better understand and manage their energy and water usage without feeling overwhelmed or confused.
